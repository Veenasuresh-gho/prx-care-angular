How to take the perfect food photo

Better photos mean more accurate nutritional analysis. Follow these simple tips to get the best results from PRx Food Scanner.

Do's

  • Use natural daylight when possible
  • Shoot from a top-down "flat lay" view
  • Ensure the plate is in clear focus
  • Keep individual ingredients visible

Don'ts

  • Avoid blurry or shaky shots
  • Don't shoot in dark, moody rooms
  • Don't hide ingredients under others
  • Avoid extreme side-angle views

Perfect example: Clear light, top-down, distinct ingredients.
